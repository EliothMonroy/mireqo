Wordmark exact lowercase text 'mireqo' (m i r e q o), readable custom humanist sans-serif, medium weight, open counters. Text consists ONLY of the small concept title and the word 'mireqo'; no extra labels/slogans. Logos should have bold compact silhouettes that could be redrawn as clean SVG and remain readable at small app-icon sizes. No gradients, textures, shadows, 3D, tiny decorative detail, trademark symbols or imitation of existing brands. These are original concept previews, not final production vector files. Concept title '02 / OPEN CITY'. Create a distinctive terracotta lowercase m monogram from two bold friendly architectural archways, like welcoming entrances to the city and its events. Solid substantial forms, two clear cream doorway openings, subtly differing arch heights, flattened baseline, confidently geometric. A recognisable m, not a map pin, not a thin continuous golden-arches outline, not an infinity sign. Capture openness and urban exploration through the monogram alone.
